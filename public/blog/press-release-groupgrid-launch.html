GroupGrid launches to catch event-travel booking gaps before check-in

For immediate release · New York, NY · September 11, 2026

GroupGrid, a web tool for event planners and corporate travel managers, is live today at groupgrid.io. GroupGrid cross-checks an event's registration list against its flight, hotel, and ground-transfer bookings and flags the gaps before the event, while they are still easy to fix.

Every event runs on separate lists: registration, flight manifests, rooming lists, transfer schedules. The costly problems hide between them. A VIP lands with no room booked. A guest flies into the wrong airport. A flight arrives the day after check-in and nobody notices until the guest is standing in the lobby. Planners have traditionally caught these by hand, reading across spreadsheets the week before a program.

GroupGrid puts every attendee on one grid and checks each record against the next. In about a minute it reports:

  • Who registered but has no booking
  • Who is booked but never registered
  • Whose flight, hotel, and transfer dates do not line up
  • Who is flying into the wrong airport or outside the approved travel window

Attendees whose records agree are cleared. The short list that needs attention is flagged with enough detail to fix in a two-line email.

"Every event has one person quietly making sure the travel all lines up," said Amanda Grandal, founder and CEO of GroupGrid. "In my first job that person was me, with a ruler and a printed rooming list. Later we outsourced it and the gaps still slipped through, just later and more expensively. The problem was never any one list. It was the space between them, and nothing was looking there. GroupGrid looks there."

Built to keep guest data private

Guest files are read and cross-checked entirely in the user's browser and are never uploaded to GroupGrid's servers. Planners upload the files they already have, in the formats they already use, with no template to fill in first.
