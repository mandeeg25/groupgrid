GroupGrid Launches New Platform to Help Event Planners Catch Travel Gaps Before Check-In

For immediate release · New York · September 14, 2026

GroupGrid, a new platform designed for event planners and corporate travel managers, officially launched today at groupgrid.io.

GroupGrid cross-checks an event’s registration list against flight, hotel, and ground transportation records, helping planners identify missing bookings, incorrect dates, and other travel discrepancies before attendees arrive.

Event travel information often lives across several disconnected sources. Registration is housed in one system, flight details arrive in another file, hotels provide rooming lists, and transportation teams maintain separate schedules. Important issues can easily hide between those lists.

GroupGrid brings every attendee together on one clear grid and checks their information across each part of the travel journey. The platform can identify attendees who registered but have no corresponding travel booking, travelers who have reservations but never registered, hotel and flight dates that do not align, and arrivals outside approved travel dates or at an incorrect airport.

Attendee records that align are cleared, allowing planners to focus on the smaller list of travelers who may require follow-up.

“Every event has someone behind the scenes making sure the travel details line up,” said Amanda Grandal, founder and CEO of GroupGrid. “Early in my career, that person was me with a ruler and a printed rooming list. Even as the process became more sophisticated, the same gaps continued to appear because every piece of information lived somewhere different. I built GroupGrid to give event planners one clear place to see what matches, what doesn’t, and what needs their attention.”

Guest files are processed directly within the user’s browser and are not uploaded to GroupGrid’s servers. Planners can upload the files they already use without first transferring the information into a required template.

“Event planners manage thousands of details, and reconciling travel should not take days,” Grandal added. “GroupGrid gives planners visibility into potential issues in minutes so they can spend their time creating a better event experience.”

GroupGrid is available as a self-service subscription at groupgrid.io. Plans are priced at $250 per user per month or $2,000 per year. Group pricing is available by contacting billing@groupgrid.io. A two-minute demo is at groupgrid.io/#demo.
